Use of Concept: metals

Description

Substances that tend to be malleable/ductile and conduct electricity and heat well

Examples:
- Common metals include aluminum, steel, iron, and copper, as well as their alloys
- Precious metals, often used for electronics, include gold, silver, platinum, or palladium
